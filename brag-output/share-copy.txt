In Antarctica, blackouts at -50°C are fatal. We built PolarGrid AI: a physics-grounded microgrid digital twin for Indian Antarctic stations (Maitri & Bharati) that pairs a benchmarked Ridge load forecaster (1.75 kW test MAE vs 2.96 kW persistence baseline) with automated 25 m/s blizzard storm furling and 55% genset wet-stacking protection. Measured against realistic rule-based heuristic dispatch, it yields 18,000 to 38,000 L/yr of verified fuel savings while guaranteeing 100% life-support uptime.
