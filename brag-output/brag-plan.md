# Brag Plan: PolarGrid AI — Launch Video
**Project**: PolarGrid AI (PS SIH26061)  
**Target Audience**: Smart India Hackathon Jury, Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES), NCPOR station directors  
**Tone**: `cinematic` + `polished` (Scientific industrial, zero hype, rigorous engineering)  
**Format**: Landscape 16:9 (1920×1080 @ 30fps)  
**Duration**: 24.0s (720 frames)  
**Deliverable Path**: `brag-output/brag.mp4`, `brag-output/brag.jpg`, `brag-output/share-copy.txt`  

---

## 1. Creative Angle & Identity
- **The Hook (0.0s – 3.0s)**: Extreme isolation. -50°C Antarctic winter, 100 km/h blizzard winds, and single annual fuel replenishment. Life support cannot afford blackouts or guessing games.
- **The Reveal (3.0s – 7.0s)**: PolarGrid AI: An advisory microgrid digital twin purpose-built for Maitri and Bharati stations.
- **Highlight 1 — Scientific Machine Learning (7.0s – 12.0s)**: Genuine Scikit-Learn Ridge model fitted on chronological train/test split. Measured 1.75 kW MAE vs 2.96 kW persistence baseline (+40.9% improvement).
- **Highlight 2 — Physical Interlocks & Wet-Stacking Protection (12.0s – 18.0s)**: Blizzard winds > 25 m/s trigger automated turbine storm furling cut-out (0 kW) with instant safety alerts. 120 kW genset clamped at 55% floor (66 kW) to eliminate bore glazing.
- **Highlight 3 — 3-Tier Priority Shedding & Grounded Savings (18.0s – 21.5s)**: Tier 1 Life Support preserved at 100%. Measured against realistic rule-based heuristic showing 8%–16% seasonal diesel savings (18k–38k L/yr).
- **Punchline & Outro (21.5s – 24.0s)**: 100% offline, zero cloud reliance. Mission ready for Indian Antarctic expeditions.
